## Description
A calm, old-school screensaver for Windows. When you step away, little pixel animals drift in one by one from the edges of your screen and wander over whatever you left open. There's no black screen and no clock, just your desktop with company.

Move the mouse or press any key and they're gone instantly.

• Seven animals: koi, butterflies, jellyfish, dragonflies, frogs, cranes and crabs, or let it rotate through all of them
• Real depth: animals in front are sharp, the ones behind are soft and blurred, and they never pile on top of each other
• Tweak speed, size, density, direction, sway and more
• Choose how long you're idle before it starts (1 to 30 minutes)
• Works across all your monitors
• Waits while a video or music is playing, and during full-screen games and presentations
• Lives quietly in the system tray and uses almost nothing while hidden
• No account, no internet, no data collected

Free and open source: https://github.com/pratikphophaliya/ascii-animals-screensaver

## Notes for certification
This is a desktop (Electron) app, so it needs runFullTrust. It runs in the system tray and shows a see-through screensaver overlay over the desktop when the user is idle. To see it right away without waiting: right-click the tray icon and choose "Preview now", then move the mouse to dismiss. No account, no network access, no data collection.
