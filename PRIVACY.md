# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

**ASCII Animals** (the screensaver app for Windows, including the Microsoft Store version) does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in.
- **No network access.** The app makes no internet connections and has no analytics, tracking, ads, or crash reporting.
- **Local settings only.** Your choices (which animal, idle delay, tweaks) are saved in a small file on your own PC and never leave it. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- **Idle and full-screen checks stay on your PC.** To know when to start, the app asks Windows how long since the last mouse or keyboard input and whether a full-screen app is in front. It doesn't record what you type, what's on screen, or which apps you use.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pratikphophaliya/ascii-animals-screensaver/issues
